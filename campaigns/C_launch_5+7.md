## Day −3 · The one-sentence spec (free lesson, no pitch)

**Subject:** Read this before you prompt anything
**Preview:** goal · inputs · steps · output · constraints.

Ciaooooo,

Fabio here.

Today: no story, no offer. Just the tool I wish someone had handed me.

Before you let any AI agent write your app, fill in five slots:

```
GOAL       — one sentence: what does the tool DO?
INPUTS     — what does it start from? (a folder, a CSV, a photo folder)
STEPS      — numbered, in plain English, what happens
OUTPUT     — what comes out, and WHERE it goes
CONSTRAINTS— what must never happen (overwrite files, need internet, use GPU)
```

That's it. A prompt with five filled slots beats an hour of vague "make me a tool" guessing. The empty slots are where the AI guesses wrong — and where your Saturday afternoon goes to die.

Try it on something embarrassingly small this week: *rename every photo in a folder by date taken. Keep them in place. Never overwrite. Skip files without dates.* Ten minutes. It works.

Tomorrow I'll tell you what I'm building for people who want to go further than one script.
