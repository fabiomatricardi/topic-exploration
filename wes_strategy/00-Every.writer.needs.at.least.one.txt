Every writer needs at least one

Hi Substacker, 
 
I think every writer who wants to earn from their writing should have at least one small digital product.
 
Not necessarily a big course. It could be a short guide, a set of templates, or a workshop that helps readers with one specific problem.
 
Here's why.
 
Your writing can help someone understand what they need to do. But they might still need help doing it.
 
A reader enjoys your post about finding a job but doesn't know what to say to a hiring manager.
 
Someone reads your advice about meal planning…but still spends Sunday trying to put a shopping list together.
 
A useful product helps them take that next step.
 
And it gives you something to offer beyond another article.
 
My first digital product was a 25-page PDF guide answering questions readers kept asking about growing on Substack.
 
I created it over a weekend. Within 72 hours, 23 people bought it for $47 each.
 
Over $1,000 from something I'd written once and could offer again.
 
After years of freelance writing and career coaching, that was a different way to earn. I hadn't taken on another client or filled another afternoon with calls.
 
I'd created something readers could use on their own.
 
You can still write your book. You can still offer paid subscriptions. But a small product gives you a way to start earning without waiting for either of those to come together.
