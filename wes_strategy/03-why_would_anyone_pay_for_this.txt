Why would anyone pay for this?

Hi Substacker, 
 
“Why would anyone pay for this?”
 
If you've thought about creating a digital product, you've probably asked yourself that question.
 
There's already a book about your topic. Someone with a bigger audience teaches it. And couldn't your readers just find the information for free?
 
It's easy to stop there and go back to writing another post.
 
But think about the last time you paid for something that made your life easier.
 
You probably could've figured it out yourself. You just didn't want to spend another weekend searching, comparing advice, and wondering whether you were doing it right.
 
Your readers feel that way, too.
 
A writer struggling to pitch publications might appreciate a few pitch templates with examples. Someone overwhelmed by meal planning might want five easy dinners and a shopping list.
 
These are digital products: small resources you sell online that help someone do something they're struggling with.
 
They don't have to cover everything you know. They need to help with one thing.
 
And what feels obvious to you might be the thing someone else has spent weeks trying to figure out.
 
I made my first $1,000 with fewer than 500 subscribers.
 
What mattered most was realizing I could earn from something I'd created without booking another client call.
 
After years of freelance writing and career coaching, that possibility meant a lot to me. 
 
If I wanted to earn more, I'd always had to fit more work into my calendar. This gave me another option.
