# Market Exploration — Finding My Next Digital Product

A working repository to **discover, validate, and launch feasible digital products** from my existing AI content (Medium + Substack), sold via kit.com. Every product must serve my reader — people in their 40s and 50s who want to **own their AI**: run it locally, for free, on the machine they already have. *"Your AI, Your Rules."*

---

## Start here (reading order for a new session)

1. **[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)** — the operating manual: rules, workflow, session checklist, build order, and the Wes-rule appendix. **Read this first.**
2. **[`research/demand-log.md`](research/demand-log.md)** — what problems have been found and scored so far (the repo's memory).
3. **[`products/next_products.md`](products/next_products.md)** — the candidate queue and what's being built next.

## Folder structure

```
.\                          AGENTS.md (operating manual) · README.md (you are here)
│
├── my_articles\            READ-ONLY · Medium articles (tone, topics, earnings_usd)
├── my_products\            READ-ONLY · already-published product copy
├── wes_strategy\           READ-ONLY · Wes Pearce's product/monetization playbook
│
├── research\               The method + everything readers told us
│   ├── README.md           Demand Discovery Playbook (sources · queries · ritual · scoring)
│   ├── demand-log.md       Logged problems: exact quotes + 5-dimension scores
│   └── reader_profile.md   The reader persona, objections, vocabulary, price points
│
├── products\               Ideas → products
│   ├── next_products.md    4 candidates (A/B/C/D) + lead magnet + decision matrix
│   └── product_C_outline.md  Full outline: "First Real App in 4 Weekends"
│
├── campaigns\              Launches
│   └── C_launch_5+7.md     12 full emails (5 prep + 7 selling) + Wes-rule appendix
│
└── outputs\                Generated assets (exports, images, scripts)
```

**Source folders** (`my_articles\`, `my_products\`, `wes_strategy\`) are reference material — never edit them. All new work goes into `research\`, `products\`, `campaigns\`, `outputs\`.

## The loop in one line

**Research (30 min/week) → log with exact quotes → score (≥16 = real) → candidate → one outline at a time → 5+7 campaign → launch → harvest replies → repeat.**

Full steps: [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) §4–§6.
